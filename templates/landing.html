About SmartJob

Bridging Candidates and Roles with Transparent AI

Smart Job Recommendation System is an open and transparent job intelligence platform. Traditional keyword search filters often overlook true talent or overwhelm job seekers with hundreds of irrelevant results.

Our platform utilizes state-of-the-art NLP, TF-IDF vectorization, and cosine similarity algorithms to pair your authentic competencies with verified company requirements. With explicit skill gap reporting, you always know where you stand and what to learn next.
